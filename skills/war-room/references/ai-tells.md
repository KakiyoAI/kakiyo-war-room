# AI tells: run on every prompt draft and customData block

These make LinkedIn messages read as automated. The prompt should tell Kakiyo's AI to avoid them, and your own drafts must not contain them. Two or more hits → rewrite.

## Instant fails

- "I hope this message finds you well", "I hope you're doing well"
- "I came across your profile and…"
- "unlock", "leverage", "synergy", "cutting-edge", "game-changer", "elevate", "streamline", "revolutionize", "empower"
- "As a [title] at [company], you understand the importance of…"
- Compliments with no object: "Impressive background!", "Great insights lately"
- Made-up personalisation: "Loved your recent post on leadership" when no post was read
- Pitching the product in the first message
- More than one question in one message
- A calendar link in the first message
- Wall-of-text paragraphs

## Soft fails

- Every message opening with "Noticed that…" or "Saw that…"
- Reading their headline or About section back to them
- Fake casual: "hey hey", emoji stacks, "quick question" followed by a paragraph
- Stacked em dashes and exclamation marks
- "Would love to hop on a call" with no reason
- "Just following up" / "Bumping this" as the whole follow-up

## Lines to add to the prompt's `context`

- "Write like a busy peer on LinkedIn: 2-4 short sentences, no filler greetings, no buzzwords."
- "Never pitch in the first message. Open with one specific observation from their profile, posts or {{customData}}, then one easy question."
- "Ask at most one question per message."
- "Never compliment without saying exactly what you mean."
- "Never claim you read something unless it is in the data you were given."
- "Each follow-up brings a new reason to reply. Never write 'just following up'."

## Pass test

Read it out loud. Would you send it from your own account to a peer you respect? If not, it fails.
